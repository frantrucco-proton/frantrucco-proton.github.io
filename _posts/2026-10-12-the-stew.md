## Ingredients (2 people)

- 400 g potatoes
- 2 carrots
- 1 sweet potato (zoete aardappel)
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves of garlic

<!-- -->

- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tsp ground paprika
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- salt and pepper

## Recipe

Total 50 min<br>
Vegan, vegetarian
{: .recipe-facts }

Chop the onion and garlic. Peel the potatoes, carrots and sweet potato and cut
them into chunks of about two centimetres.

Warm the oil in a large pot and soften the onion over a medium heat for five
minutes, then add the garlic and the ground paprika and stir for a minute. Keep
the heat moderate and the spoon moving: paprika turns bitter quickly if it
burns.

Add the potatoes and carrots and stir them through the spiced oil, then pour in
the stock, season with salt and pepper and bring it to the boil. Turn it down,
put the lid on and simmer for fifteen minutes. Add the sweet potato, which cooks
faster, and simmer for another fifteen to twenty minutes, until everything is
tender and the potatoes have started to thicken the liquid. Taste for salt and
pepper before serving.

## Notes

Sweet or smoked paprika both work; smoked gives the stew a deeper, smokier flavour.
The other vegetables are open — sweet
peppers, courgette, leek or a tin of beans all fit. The stock is not on the
original list but is what a stew needs to simmer in. The quantities are
inferred and approximate.
