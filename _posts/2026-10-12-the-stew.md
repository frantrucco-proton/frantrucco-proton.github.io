## Ingredients (2 people)

- 2 sweet peppers
- 400 g potatoes
- 2 carrots
- 1 sweet potato (zoete aardappel)
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves of garlic

<!-- -->

- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp sweet or smoked paprika powder (paprikapoeder)
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- salt and pepper

## Recipe

Total 50 min<br>
Vegan, vegetarian
{: .recipe-facts }

Chop the onion and garlic. Peel the potatoes, carrots and sweet potato and cut
them into chunks of about two centimetres, and cut the peppers into strips.

Warm the oil in a large pot and soften the onion over a medium heat for five
minutes, then add the garlic and the paprika powder and stir for a minute
without letting it burn.

Add the potatoes and carrots and stir them through the oil, then pour in the
stock, season with salt and pepper and bring it to the boil. Turn it down, put
the lid on and simmer for fifteen minutes. Add the sweet potato and peppers,
which cook faster, and simmer for another fifteen to twenty minutes, until
everything is tender and the potatoes have started to thicken the liquid. Taste
for salt and pepper before serving.

## Notes

The paprika on the list is read as sweet peppers, which is what the word means
on a Dutch shelf; the spoon of paprika powder is an addition, and can be left
out. The other vegetables are open — courgette, leek or a tin of beans all fit.
The stock is not on the original list but is what a stew needs to simmer in. The
quantities are inferred and approximate.
