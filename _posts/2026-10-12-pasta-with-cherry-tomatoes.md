## Ingredients (2 people)

- 200 g pasta, such as spaghetti or penne, dry weight
- 400 g cherry tomatoes
- 40 g grated Parmesan-style hard cheese, rennet-free (vegetarisch)

<!-- -->

- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves of garlic
- salt and pepper

## Recipe

Total 20 min<br>
Vegetarian
{: .recipe-facts }

Bring a large pot of water to the boil, salt it well and cook the pasta for the
time on the packet. While it cooks, halve the cherry tomatoes and thinly slice
the garlic.

Warm the olive oil in a wide frying pan over a medium heat, add the garlic and
let it sizzle for a minute without browning. Tip in the tomatoes with a pinch of
salt and cook them for five to eight minutes, stirring now and then, until they
collapse and their juice turns into a loose sauce.

Before you drain the pasta, scoop out a mug of the cooking water. Add the pasta
to the pan with a splash of that water and toss it over the heat for a minute,
so the starchy water and the oil come together and coat every piece. Take it off
the heat, stir in half the cheese and season with pepper, then serve with the
rest of the cheese on top.

## Notes

Real Parmesan is made with animal rennet, so this page uses a vegetarian
Parmesan-style hard cheese instead, labelled `vegetarisch`; it is used exactly
the same way. The garlic is not on the original list but is in almost every
version of this dish; leave it out if you prefer. The quantities are inferred
and approximate.
