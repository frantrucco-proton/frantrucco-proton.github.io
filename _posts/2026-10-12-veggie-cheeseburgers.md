## Ingredients (2 people)

- 2 burger buns (hamburgerbroodjes)
- 2 vegetarian burger patties (vegetarische burgers)
- 2 slices of cheese, labelled vegetarian (vegetarisch)
- 1 or 2 tomatoes
- a few lettuce leaves

<!-- -->

- 1 tbsp oil
- burger sauce, or ketchup and mayonnaise

## Recipe

Total 15 min<br>
Vegetarian
{: .recipe-facts }

Slice the tomatoes and wash the lettuce. Cut the buns open and toast them, cut
side down, in a dry frying pan for a minute until golden, then set them aside.

Heat the oil in the same pan over a medium heat and fry the patties for the time
on the packet, usually three to five minutes a side. For the last minute, lay a
slice of cheese on each and cover the pan with a lid so it melts.

Spread sauce on both halves of each bun, then build the burger: lettuce, the
patty with its cheese, then tomato, and the top of the bun.

## Notes

The original uses beef cheeseburgers. Every recipe on this site is vegetarian, so
vegetarian burger patties replace the beef; they cook the same way, but follow
the packet times, which vary by brand. Much Dutch and hard cheese is made with
animal rennet, so pick slices marked `vegetarisch`. Some burger sauces contain
Worcestershire sauce, which has anchovies; check the label, or mix ketchup and
mayonnaise instead. The quantities are inferred and approximate.
