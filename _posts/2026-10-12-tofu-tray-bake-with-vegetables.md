## Ingredients (2 people)

- 1 block of firm tofu, about 375 g (tofu naturel, stevig)
- 1 bag of mixed vegetables for the oven, about 600 g (ovengroenten)
- 1 lemon

<!-- -->

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
- salt and pepper

## Recipe

Total 35 min<br>
Vegan with maple syrup, vegetarian
{: .recipe-facts }

Heat the oven to 200 °C. Drain the tofu, wrap it in a clean tea towel and press
it under something heavy for ten minutes while the oven warms up; the drier it
is, the better it browns. Then cut it into thick slices, or cubes if you prefer.

Tip the vegetables onto a baking tray, toss them with two tablespoons of the
olive oil, salt and pepper, and spread them out in one layer. Roast them for ten
minutes on their own, since most of them take longer than the tofu.

Meanwhile stir together the soy sauce, honey, the last tablespoon of oil and the
juice of half the lemon, and turn the tofu in it. Make room among the vegetables,
lay the tofu on the tray and pour any remaining marinade over it. Roast for
another fifteen to twenty minutes, until the vegetables are soft and catching at
the edges and the tofu is golden. Serve with the rest of the lemon in wedges.

## Notes

The original is salmon roasted with a bag of vegetables. Every recipe on this
site is vegetarian, so firm tofu replaces the salmon; it goes onto the tray at
the same point the fish would, about ten minutes in, and the soy and lemon
marinade gives it the flavour the salmon would otherwise bring. Smoked tofu
(gerookte tofu) also works and needs no marinade. The quantities are inferred
and approximate.
