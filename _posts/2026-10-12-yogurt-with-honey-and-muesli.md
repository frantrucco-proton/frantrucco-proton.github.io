## Ingredients (2 people)

- 400 g Greek or Turkish yogurt (Griekse of Turkse yoghurt)
- 80 g muesli

<!-- -->

- 2 tbsp honey

## Recipe

Total 2 min<br>
Vegetarian
{: .recipe-facts }

Spoon the yogurt into two bowls, scatter the muesli over the top and drizzle the
honey over everything. Eat it straight away, while the muesli still has some
crunch; left to sit, it soaks up the yogurt and goes soft.
